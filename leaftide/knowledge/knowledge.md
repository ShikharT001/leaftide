## About the Brand

- Leaftide Tea Co. was founded in 2018 in Darjeeling, India.
- The company is a small, family-run business with a team of twelve people.
- Leaftide sources all its tea leaves directly from three partner farms in Darjeeling, Assam, and Nilgiri.
- The brand's mission is to make single-origin teas accessible without compromising on quality.

## Products

- Leaftide currently offers six teas: Darjeeling First Flush, Assam Bold Breakfast, Nilgiri Frost, Jasmine Silver Needle, Roasted Hojicha, and Chamomile Calm.
- Darjeeling First Flush is a light, floral black tea harvested in March–April. It has a muscatel character.
- Assam Bold Breakfast is a full-bodied, malty black tea. It pairs well with milk.
- Nilgiri Frost is a medium-bodied black tea with a bright, citrusy finish, grown at 2,000 metres elevation.
- Jasmine Silver Needle is a white tea scented with fresh jasmine blossoms. It is the brand's most delicate offering.
- Roasted Hojicha is a Japanese-style roasted green tea with a nutty, low-caffeine profile.
- Chamomile Calm is a caffeine-free herbal blend with dried chamomile and a hint of lemon balm.

## Pricing and Packaging

- All teas are sold in 50 g tins priced between ₹450 and ₹850.
- Darjeeling First Flush is the most expensive at ₹850 per 50 g tin.
- Chamomile Calm is the most affordable at ₹450 per 50 g tin.

## Brewing Guidance

- Black teas (Darjeeling, Assam, Nilgiri) should be brewed at 90–95 °C for 3–4 minutes.
- White and jasmine teas should be brewed at 75–80 °C for 2–3 minutes to avoid bitterness.
- Hojicha can be brewed at 90 °C for 1–2 minutes. It can also be enjoyed cold-brewed overnight.
- Chamomile Calm should be steeped at 95–100 °C for 5 minutes for full flavour.
